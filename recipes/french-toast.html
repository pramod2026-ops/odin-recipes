Golden and Delicious Breakfast

French toast is made by dipping bread into a sweet egg and milk mixture before cooking it until golden brown.

Serve it with fruit, honey, syrup, or powdered sugar for a delicious breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 4 slices bread
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Honey or syrup

Steps:

  1. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, and cinnamon together.
  2. Dip each slice of bread into the mixture.
  3. Heat butter in a pan.
  4. Cook the bread until golden brown.
  5. Flip and cook the other side.
  6. Repeat with remaining bread.
  7. Serve warm with honey or syrup.
