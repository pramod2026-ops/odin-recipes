Golden and Crispy Potato Snack

French fries are crispy potato strips that make a perfect snack or side dish.

They can be served with ketchup, mayonnaise, or your favorite dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • 4 large potatoes
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika

Steps:

  1. Peel and cut the potatoes into thin strips.
  2. Soak the potato strips in cold water.
  3. Drain and dry the potatoes thoroughly.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pan.
  5. Fry the potatoes until golden and crispy.
  6. Drain on paper towels.
  7. Season with salt and spices.
  8. Serve immediately.
