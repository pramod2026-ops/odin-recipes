Simple and Delicious Italian Pasta

Spaghetti is a classic pasta dish made with noodles, tomato sauce, herbs, and cheese. It is simple, filling, and perfect for a quick dinner.

This beginner-friendly recipe takes about 30 minutes and can easily be customized with vegetables or meat.

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • Salt to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

Steps:

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
  2. Cook spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan.
  4. Add minced garlic and cook briefly.
  5. Add tomato sauce and oregano.
  6. Drain the spaghetti and add it to the sauce.
  7. Mix well and serve with Parmesan cheese.
