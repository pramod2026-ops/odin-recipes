Crispy and Buttery Garlic Bread

Garlic bread is a simple side dish made with bread, butter, garlic, and herbs.

It pairs especially well with pasta, soup, or salad.

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf French bread
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cut the bread into slices.
  3. Mix butter, garlic, parsley, and salt.
  4. Spread the garlic butter over the bread.
  5. Sprinkle Parmesan cheese on top.
  6. Place the bread on a baking tray.
  7. Bake until golden and crispy.
  8. Serve warm.
