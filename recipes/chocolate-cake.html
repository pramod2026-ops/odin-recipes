Rich and Moist Chocolate Dessert

Chocolate cake is a classic dessert with a soft texture and rich chocolate flavor.

It is perfect for birthdays, celebrations, or simply enjoying with a cup of tea.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and baking powder.
  3. In another bowl, mix eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla.
  4. Combine the wet and dry ingredients.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  6. Bake for about 30 minutes.
  7. Allow the cake to cool before serving.
