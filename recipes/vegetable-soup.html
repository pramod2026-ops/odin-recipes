Vegetable soup is a simple and nutritious dish made with fresh vegetables and flavorful broth.

It is perfect for a light lunch or a warm dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 carrot
  • 1 potato
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/2 cup cabbage
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a large pot.
  2. Add chopped onion and cook until soft.
  3. Add the remaining vegetables.
  4. Pour in the vegetable broth.
  5. Bring the soup to a boil.
  6. Reduce heat and simmer until vegetables are tender.
  7. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. Serve hot.
