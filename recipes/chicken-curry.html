Rich and Flavorful Chicken Dish

Chicken curry is a comforting dish made with tender chicken, onions, tomatoes, and aromatic spices.

It pairs wonderfully with steamed rice, roti, or naan and makes a satisfying family meal.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add chopped onions and cook until golden.
  3. Add garlic and ginger.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and spices.
  5. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  6. Add chicken and mix well.
  7. Add water and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked.
  8. Serve hot with rice or roti.
