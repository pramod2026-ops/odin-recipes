Creamy and Comforting Pasta

Creamy pasta is a rich and satisfying dish made with pasta, cream, garlic, and Parmesan cheese.

This simple recipe makes a delicious dinner in around 25 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 teaspoon parsley

Steps:

  1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water.
  2. Melt butter in a pan.
  3. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  4. Pour in the cream.
  5. Add Parmesan cheese and stir.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Add the cooked pasta and mix well.
  8. Garnish with parsley and serve.
