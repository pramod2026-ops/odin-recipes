Fresh and Colorful Healthy Snack

Fruit salad is a refreshing combination of fresh fruits that is naturally sweet and easy to prepare.

It makes a healthy breakfast, snack, or dessert.

Ingredients

  • 1 apple
  • 1 banana
  • 1 orange
  • 1 cup grapes
  • 1 cup strawberries
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

Steps:

  1. Wash all the fruits.
  2. Peel and cut the fruits into small pieces.
  3. Place all fruits in a large bowl.
  4. Add honey and lemon juice.
  5. Gently mix everything together.
  6. Chill in the refrigerator if desired.
  7. Serve fresh.
