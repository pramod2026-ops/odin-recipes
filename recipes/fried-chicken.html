Crispy and Juicy Chicken

Fried chicken is a popular comfort food with a crispy seasoned coating and juicy chicken inside.

It is great for family meals and can be served with salad, fries, or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken pieces
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps:

  1. Season the chicken with salt and spices.
  2. Mix egg and milk in a bowl.
  3. Place flour and seasonings in another bowl.
  4. Dip chicken into the egg mixture.
  5. Coat the chicken with seasoned flour.
  6. Heat oil in a deep pan.
  7. Fry chicken until golden and fully cooked.
  8. Drain on paper towels and serve.
