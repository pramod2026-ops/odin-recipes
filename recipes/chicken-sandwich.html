Easy and Filling Chicken Sandwich

This chicken sandwich combines seasoned chicken with fresh vegetables and creamy sauce between slices of bread.

It is perfect for lunch and can be prepared quickly.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 150g cooked chicken
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 2 tomato slices
  • 1 tablespoon mayonnaise
  • 1 teaspoon mustard
  • Salt and pepper

Steps:

  1. Cook and season the chicken.
  2. Toast the bread lightly.
  3. Spread mayonnaise and mustard on the bread.
  4. Add lettuce and tomato.
  5. Place the cooked chicken on top.
  6. Add the second slice of bread.
  7. Cut the sandwich and serve.
