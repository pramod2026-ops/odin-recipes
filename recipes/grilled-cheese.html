Crispy Bread and Melty Cheese

Grilled cheese is a simple sandwich made with crispy golden bread and melted cheese.

It is quick to prepare and makes a great lunch or snack.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread
  • 2 slices cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Pinch of black pepper

Steps:

  1. Butter one side of each bread slice.
  2. Place cheese between the unbuttered sides.
  3. Heat a pan over medium heat.
  4. Place the sandwich in the pan.
  5. Cook until the bread becomes golden.
  6. Flip and cook the other side.
  7. Serve while hot.
