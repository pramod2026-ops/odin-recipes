Soft and Sweet Homemade Bread

Banana bread is a delicious way to use ripe bananas. It has a soft texture and naturally sweet banana flavor.

This simple recipe is beginner-friendly and makes a wonderful snack.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • Pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mash the bananas in a bowl.
  3. Add melted butter and sugar.
  4. Mix in eggs and vanilla.
  5. Add flour, baking soda, and salt.
  6. Mix until just combined.
  7. Pour into a loaf pan.
  8. Bake for about 50 minutes.
  9. Cool before slicing and serving.
