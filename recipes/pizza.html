Homemade pizza is a delicious combination of crispy dough, tomato sauce, melted cheese, and your favorite toppings.

This recipe is easy to customize and is perfect for a weekend meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings
  • Pinch of salt

Steps:

  1. Mix flour, yeast, salt, water, and oil.
  2. Knead until a smooth dough forms.
  3. Let the dough rest for about one hour.
  4. Roll the dough into a pizza shape.
  5. Spread tomato sauce over the dough.
  6. Add cheese and toppings.
  7. Bake until the crust is golden and cheese is melted.
  8. Slice and serve.
